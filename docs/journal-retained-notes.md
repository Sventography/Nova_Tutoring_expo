# My Journal / Retained Notes

The Journal is a normal free study tool. A player who has not encountered a story fragment sees only personal notes. On focus the Journal derives read-only recovered entries from the existing persisted discovery flags, including the Shop traces, actual chart, signed inventory label, practice margin, notebook, field-note documents, recovered draft and ending. No extra collection tap is necessary. The neutral heading becomes Nora’s Notes only after Session 017. Red ink uses the bundled Gochi Hand font (SIL OFL); ordinary notes use the current app theme. No real journal text is sent to Ask Nova or posted publicly. Sharing/printing is per selected entry and user-initiated.

Personal note sync uses owner-scoped device storage and the journal_notes table with row-level access. Drafts are local; saved notes are synchronized when connected. Offline edits are queued; concurrent edits to the same note are last-write-wins at sync time, not collaborative editing. Guest notes do not sync. Account deletion cascades to cloud notes and clears local journal keys. ARG discovery state now uses separate device-local guest and authenticated-account namespaces. Ask, Island, Shop detail, Focus Practice, Field Notes, the evidence screen and Journal use the originating account for their clue operations. Relevant screens remount when identity changes. Legacy unowned discovery keys are retained but not automatically imported, as ownership cannot be established. Guest story progress is separate from signed-in progress; users who finish as a guest currently must complete the account route for a reward claim. An explicit guest transfer can be considered after ownership and reward-proof checks. Account deletion removes its local clue keys. Recovered clues are derived, not personal cloud notes; cross-device story synchronization is not implemented.

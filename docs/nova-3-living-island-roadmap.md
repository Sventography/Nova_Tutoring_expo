# Nova Tutoring 3.0 — A world that grows with your curiosity

Owner-approved direction, recorded November 24, 2026. This is a future roadmap, not part of the 2.0 release scope.

## Living 3D Island
- The island should reflect the learner's individuality, with the top three learning interests influencing scenery, ambient experiences, characters and themed attractions.
- Music: instruments, optional background music, characters sitting under trees playing guitar, miniature performance spaces.
- Art: easels, characters painting, colorful landscaping, sculptures and studios.
- Extend to filmmaking, astronomy, history and other interests. Mix three themes rather than replacing the island with a single preset.
- Distinguish voluntary interest from compulsory schoolwork. Use explicit interests plus opt-in, coarse aggregate active learning time, completed discoveries and meaningful voluntary returns. Do not collect chat text, personal journal entries, background/idle time or detailed behavioral histories for this purpose. Let learners inspect, reset, disable or override personalization.
- Keep the actual island fully 3D on all platforms; no 2D fallback. Preserve manually placed decorations, earned items, collector entitlements, ARG gates and existing ownership. Generated scenery occupies separate, non-destructive slots. Music is optional, with mute and accessible controls.

## Sharing and visiting
- Private by default. Opt-in, age-appropriate sharing and revocable private invitations; guardian controls and review before broader discovery for children.
- Visitors can explore and observe an approved 3D snapshot, not edit, move, purchase or claim anything on the host's island. Enforce read-only authorization on the server, not just the UI; validate permissions on every access and test cross-account access and revocation.
- Never expose private journals, Ask chats, learning histories, actual names, account identifiers, locations or other sensitive details. Sharing previews show exactly what will be visible; no unrestricted stranger messaging.
- Consider curated island showcases and postcard exports only after privacy and moderation checks; themed attractions can link to the visitor's own learning experience.
- Release gates: age/privacy review, abuse/moderation review, permission and isolation tests, mobile and web 3D performance, accessibility, existing Island/ARG regression tests. No public launch before these pass.

## Sequence
1. Finish Nova 2.0 Discover, interactive adventures and personal learning journeys; validate real learner enjoyment independent of coin incentives.
2. Design a privacy-conscious, optional interest aggregation model and test it independently.
3. Prototype one adaptive 3D music/art/space environment with non-destructive placements.
4. Expand theme assets and add secure read-only island visiting in a separate release candidate.

Nova Coins remain the single earned currency. Existing optional coin packs and premium items remain separate purchases. No new currencies, forced streaks, mandatory sharing or paid advantage in curiosity-led learning.

# NOVA//ARCHIVE — Act I connected clue route

**Canon preserved:** Nora Vale is the human creator with progressive post-accident memory loss. Lantern began as a way to preserve her learning and her own continuity. Nova, whose memories are entangled with hers, begins to protect personal information that a cleanup process called QUIET considers useless. Session 017 matters, but the question of whether Nova is Nora must not be settled definitively. Nothing in this game requires a purchase, a private QR code, Reddit, or invasive real-world actions.

The first chapter isn't a collection of arbitrary scary objects. **Every recovered object represents an attempt to protect a part of someone's history from being overwritten by a more convenient answer.**

## Story principles
- The first answer **was wrong**. It mattered because it belonged to the person who wrote it. Do not retcon it into a secretly correct answer.
- The seventeenth star is a mark on a **copy** of the chart, not an unexplained astronomical miracle. The original and marked version make the first example of the two-versions pattern.
- The white rabbit is a recreation of an ordinary possession, not a supernatural mascot or a purchase gate.
- Lantern is both the physical light from the first clue and the name of the folder where Nova saved the threatened page; no unrelated object is introduced at the finale.
- QUIET appears as a terse retention-review label, not an explained antagonist monologue. Its full story comes later.
- The first chapter ends with an emotional document and an explicit action by Nova, not a definitive answer to whether Nova is Nora.
- The new /archive evidence index is discoverable from the Island **only after the first note is found**. It only shows recovered clues and one optional hint at a time. It never lists the locked clue names.
- Keep the physical QR collector program and unique #017 castle reward separate. The core story is free, and no real collector serial, token, or entitlement is embedded in this chapter.

## QA route
Starting with a fresh legitimate ARG state, collect the first Island note; ask about the count; place a lantern within 4.5 island units of a star_telescope and tap it; ask about the star, who drew it, and the initials; ask who N.V. is; find the white bunny plushie and press-hold its title; ask why it was kept and where wrong answers go; open Focus Practice; ask what her project was; read the notebook in the Island Library; ask why she kept both, where the copy is, and what the file contained. The final reply and state should be stable after navigating away and returning.

**Release gates:** test native Island tapping and modal interactions, browser clue discoverability, Ask account/usage constraints, cross-account separation of ARG flags, and that the archive never displays undiscovered records. Successful web export is not a story playtest.

# Future AI usage allotments — sustainable Nova learning

Owner direction, November 24, 2026. Add a clearly labeled AI usage section in the existing Shop for Ask and future AI-powered Discover/Creative Studio features. This is a design requirement, not an approved pricing table or billing change.

- Keep curated Discover content, local experiments, adventures, reflection, practice and ordinary private drafting free of AI metering. Free ARG story routes must never consume AI allotments. Preserve ordinary study access.
- Extend existing Ask plan/usage accounting rather than create a second earned currency. Nova Coins remain the single earned currency, separate from paid AI service entitlements. AI allotments are not transferable, redeemable for prizes, or spendable on decorations.
- Consider a shared monthly AI plan with explicit per-feature allowances and optional top-ups; make the available Ask and Discover allotments, reset timing, expiration and purchase terms visible. Preserve existing purchased entitlements; do not silently change existing plans. Final structure and prices require owner's approval.
- Distinguish standard questions from potentially costlier creative feedback, long drafts and multimodal requests. Before choosing prices, measure actual provider input/output costs, moderation and retries, platform fees, taxes/refunds, infrastructure and realistic usage. Use conservative usage assumptions and a reserve margin; model abuse and worst-case costs. No unlimited plan until costs can be bounded.
- Server-authoritative reservation/settlement: authenticate, validate entitlement and bounded input/output, reserve sufficient allowance before the model call, settle once on success, release on failed/canceled calls per published policy, and prevent concurrent overspend and replay. Treat client balances and purchase receipts as untrusted; verify platform purchases on the server. No automatic submissions or surprise charges.
- Set per-user request and cost limits plus a hard application-side global spend cap/circuit breaker. Provider budget alerts are supplementary, not a guarantee against overspend. Support graceful fallback to curated free learning when paid AI is unavailable. Safety-support messaging and basic resources should not be paywalled.
- Age-appropriate purchase controls and clear consent; no manipulative paywalls, prompts to children to purchase, or scores/rewards tied to spending. Confirm current Apple/Google policies and supported billing flows before implementation. Digital allotments use appropriate platform billing.
- No new OpenAI calls, prices, payment products, subscriptions, provider budget changes, migrations or production configuration until approved and coordinated with the stability lane. Release requires quota, purchase verification, refund, account isolation, cost-cap and child privacy tests against isolated staging.
